Capability Statement

Professional design, drafting, estimating, and project support capability.

Company profile

Perspective Designs & Estimates

PDE is a Saint Lucia based design, drafting, estimating, quantity surveying, visualization, and project support business. The company helps clients move from early ideas to organized drawings, realistic cost information, and clearer construction decisions.

The business is positioned to support residential projects, commercial upgrades, renovations, contractor pricing packages, development planning, and visual presentation work.

Capability workflow

How PDE supports project delivery

The process is organized to improve clarity from first discussion to final project package.

01

Review

Review available project information, goals, drawings, photos, measurements, and deadlines.

02

Define

Clarify service scope, assumptions, missing information, and the required deliverables.

03

Develop

Prepare drawings, quantities, estimates, visuals, schedules, or project support documents.

04

Coordinate

Support revisions, contractor questions, scope clarification, and package coordination.

05

Deliver

Issue a clear final package for client review, pricing, planning, or presentation.
