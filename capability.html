Company profile

Perspective Designs & Estimates

PDE is a Saint Lucia based design, drafting, estimating, and project support business. The company provides practical documentation services for residential, commercial, infrastructure, and development-related projects.

The business is positioned to support clients who need clear drawings, accurate quantities, organized project scopes, professional presentation material, and realistic budgeting information.

Core Competencies

  • Architectural drafting
  • Building design support
  • Quantity surveying
  • BOQs and material lists
  • Cost estimating
  • 3D visualization

Differentiators

  • Design and cost support in one workflow
  • Clear trade-by-trade documentation
  • Practical construction understanding
  • Professional visual presentation
  • Flexible support for clients and contractors
  • Caribbean-focused project knowledge

Industries Served

  • Residential construction
  • Commercial buildings
  • Renovations and upgrades
  • Contractor tendering
  • Development planning
  • Small infrastructure works
