What PDE does

Practical drawings, clear estimates, and better project control.

PDE brings together building design, technical drafting, quantity surveying, cost estimating, material take-offs, and project support. The goal is to help clients understand what they are building, what it may cost, and what documents are needed to move forward with confidence.

Whether the project is a residential renovation, a new home, a commercial space, or a contractor pricing package, the focus remains the same: accuracy, clarity, and professional presentation.

Simple process

How a typical project moves forward

01

Consultation

Discuss the project goals, site conditions, budget expectations, timeline, and required deliverables.

02

Concept & Scope

Prepare initial layouts, service scope, assumptions, and a clear direction for the design or estimate.

03

Documentation

Develop drawings, BOQs, material lists, details, renderings, or project packages based on the agreed scope.

04

Review & Support

Revise, clarify, and support the client with final documents, pricing checks, and contractor coordination.
