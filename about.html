About PDE

Design with vision. Build with confidence.

Perspective Designs & Estimates supports clients with professional design, drafting, estimating, quantity surveying, visualization, and project coordination services from early idea to construction documentation.

Company overview

A practical partner for design and cost clarity.

Founded in 2017, Perspective Designs & Estimates was created to help clients bridge the gap between design ideas, technical drawings, construction quantities, and realistic cost planning. PDE provides support to homeowners, developers, contractors, business owners, and organizations that need clear project information before committing to construction.

The company combines architectural drafting, building design, quantity surveying, cost estimating, material take-offs, 3D visualization, and project support. This gives clients a more complete understanding of the design, the scope of work, and the likely construction requirements.

PDE’s work is focused on practical results. Every drawing, estimate, BOQ, material list, or visual presentation is prepared to help the client make better decisions, communicate clearly with contractors, and move the project forward with confidence.

What makes PDE different

Design thinking combined with quantity and cost awareness.

Many projects struggle because design ideas, drawings, quantities, and budgets are treated separately. PDE helps bring these areas together so clients can better understand both the appearance and the practical construction requirements of a project.

  • Clear drawings that communicate the design intent.
  • Structured estimates and BOQs that support cost decisions.
  • Material lists that help with procurement and planning.
  • 3D visuals that make the project easier to understand.
  • Practical support for reviews, revisions, and contractor coordination.
  • Caribbean-focused project understanding for local construction conditions.
