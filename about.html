About PDE

Design with vision. Build with confidence.

Perspective Designs & Estimates supports clients with professional design, drafting, estimating, and project coordination services from early idea to construction documentation.

Company overview

A practical partner for design and cost clarity.

Founded in 2017, Perspective Designs & Estimates was created to help clients bridge the gap between design ideas, technical drawings, and construction cost planning. PDE provides support to homeowners, developers, contractors, and organizations that need clear documents before committing to construction.

The company’s work combines architectural drafting, building design, quantity surveying, estimating, material take-offs, 3D visualization, and project support. This allows clients to see the design, understand the scope, and make better financial decisions.
