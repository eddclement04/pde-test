Services

Design, estimating, and documentation support for better project decisions.

PDE provides services that help clients develop their ideas, prepare professional drawings, estimate costs, and organize construction information.

01

Architectural Drafting

Preparation of planning drawings, floor plans, elevations, sections, roof plans, details, schedules, and presentation sheets for residential and commercial projects.

  • Existing and proposed layouts
  • Planning and permit-style drawing packages
  • Renovation and extension documentation
02

Building Design

Concept design and design development support for new homes, commercial spaces, renovations, and façade improvements.

  • Space planning and layout options
  • Modern façade and exterior concepts
  • Design coordination with project requirements
03

Quantity Surveying & BOQs

Measured quantities and structured cost documents to help clients price works, compare contractor submissions, and manage scope.

  • Full construction BOQs
  • Material lists and quantity take-offs
  • Budget estimates and cost planning
04

3D Visualization

Visual presentations that help clients understand the look and feel of the project before construction begins.

  • Exterior concept renders
  • Interior layout visualization
  • Design upgrade previews
05

Project Support

Practical support during design review, contractor pricing, site queries, and project coordination.

  • Scope clarification
  • Drawing revisions and coordination
  • Contractor query support
06

Graphic & Presentation Support

Clean presentation material for proposals, project boards, capability statements, and development packages.

  • Presentation sheets
  • Project profiles
  • Business capability material
