How PDE supports you

Clear project information from concept to construction support.

PDE brings design, drafting, estimating, quantity surveying, and visualization into one practical workflow. This helps clients avoid confusion, compare options, understand scope, and communicate clearly with contractors, approval agencies, and project stakeholders.

Main services

Detailed service offerings

Each service can be provided individually or combined into a complete design, estimating, and project support package.

01

Architectural Drafting

Architectural drafting converts ideas, sketches, site information, and design decisions into clear drawings that can be reviewed, priced, coordinated, and used to guide construction planning.

This service is useful for new buildings, renovations, extensions, commercial fit-outs, proposed layouts, and documentation packages where clear drawings are required before moving forward.

What it can include

  • Floor plans and roof plans
  • Elevations and sections
  • Door, window, and finish schedules
  • Construction notes and details

Best for

  • Planning applications
  • Renovation documentation
  • Contractor pricing
  • Clear project communication
02

Building Design

Building design support helps shape the layout, appearance, function, and design direction of a project before detailed documentation begins. PDE focuses on practical design solutions that balance space, cost, appearance, and buildability.

This service can help clients test layout options, improve circulation, modernize a façade, plan a residential unit, or develop a concept for a commercial or mixed-use project.

What it can include

  • Concept layouts
  • Space planning
  • Exterior façade concepts
  • Design development support

Best for

  • New homes
  • Building upgrades
  • Rental unit planning
  • Commercial layouts
03

Quantity Surveying, BOQs & Cost Estimating

Quantity surveying and estimating help clients understand the amount of work involved, the materials required, and the likely cost direction of a project. This is one of the most important steps before accepting a contractor price or committing to construction.

PDE can prepare structured BOQs, quantity take-offs, cost plans, pricing schedules, and estimate support documents for residential, commercial, and contractor tendering projects.

What it can include

  • Measured BOQs
  • Trade-by-trade quantities
  • Budget estimates
  • Pricing review support

Best for

  • Client budgeting
  • Contractor pricing
  • Tender comparison
  • Scope control
04

Material Lists & Procurement Support

Material lists help clients and contractors understand what materials are required for the project and support better purchasing, planning, and cost control. A clear material list can reduce confusion when ordering materials or comparing supplier prices.

PDE can prepare material schedules from drawings, BOQs, or project descriptions, separating items into practical categories for procurement and planning.

What it can include

  • Concrete and blockwork materials
  • Finishes and painting items
  • Doors, windows, and fixtures
  • Trade-based material breakdowns

Best for

  • Purchasing planning
  • Supplier pricing
  • Contractor coordination
  • Budget tracking
05

3D Visualization

3D visualization helps clients see the project before construction. It is especially useful when deciding on exterior finishes, interior layouts, roof styles, façade upgrades, materials, lighting feel, and general presentation.

PDE can prepare visual concepts for residential, commercial, renovation, and development projects so clients can better understand the design and make informed decisions before spending on construction.

What it can include

  • Exterior render concepts
  • Interior visualization
  • Before-and-after upgrade visuals
  • Material and colour studies

Best for

  • Client presentations
  • Design approvals
  • Renovation planning
  • Marketing visuals
06

Project Support & Coordination

Project support helps clients stay organized when drawings, estimates, contractor questions, revisions, and site decisions need to be coordinated. PDE can assist with practical review and documentation support during planning, pricing, or construction preparation.

This service is helpful when clients need clarification notes, revised drawings, scope checks, contractor query support, or a more organized project package.

What it can include

  • Scope clarification
  • Drawing revisions
  • Contractor query support
  • Project documentation review

Best for

  • Project planning
  • Contractor coordination
  • Design changes
  • Construction preparation
07

Presentation & Development Support

Presentation support helps projects look organized and professional when they need to be shared with clients, investors, stakeholders, approval agencies, or internal decision-makers.

PDE can prepare project profiles, concept sheets, capability material, development summaries, and presentation boards that combine drawings, visuals, scope information, and cost-related notes.

What it can include

  • Project presentation sheets
  • Development concept summaries
  • Capability statements
  • Graphic layout support

Best for

  • Development proposals
  • Client presentations
  • Approval support packages
  • Business project profiles
Common deliverables

Documents and packages PDE can prepare

The final deliverables depend on the project scope, but these are common items clients request.

Drawing Sets

Plans, elevations, sections, details, notes, schedules, and presentation sheets for review, pricing, and planning.

BOQ Packages

Trade-by-trade quantities, descriptions, units, assumptions, clarification notes, and pricing structure.

Material Lists

Detailed material breakdowns organized by trade to support purchasing, procurement, and supplier pricing.

Cost Estimates

Budget estimates, preliminary cost plans, and pricing support documents for early decision-making.

Concept Visuals

Rendered images, exterior concepts, interior previews, material studies, and design upgrade visuals.

Project Profiles

Professional presentation material for proposals, development packages, company profiles, and client review.
